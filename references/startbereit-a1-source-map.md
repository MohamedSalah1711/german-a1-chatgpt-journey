## Suggested Daily Flow By Source

Use the PDF order as the main spine:

1. Foundation: alphabet, pronunciation, articles, pronouns, greetings.
2. Sentence engine: present tense, common verbs, family, numbers.
3. Description: negation, appearance, character, clothing.
4. Questions: W-questions, yes/no questions, prices, objects.
5. Everyday needs: modal verbs, food, routines.
6. Objects and plans: accusative, time, hobbies, invitations.
7. Places: dative, hotel, city, directions.
8. Rules and movement: separable verbs, transport, obligations.
9. Time and travel: temporal prepositions, ordinal numbers.
10. Past basics: Perfekt.
11. Wishes and comparison.
12. Health and commands.

## How To Use This Map

At the start of each new day:

1. Choose one grammar topic from the current unit.
2. Review one older topic.
3. Add exactly 10 verbs.
4. Practice with one mini-dialogue or writing task.
5. Update progress and mistakes.
